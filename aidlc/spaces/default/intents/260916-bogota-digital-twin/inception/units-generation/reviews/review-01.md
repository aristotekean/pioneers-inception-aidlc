| R-01 | Major | `unit-of-work.md` > Unit Table row U4 / U4 ingestion notes | U4 (`u4-ingestion`) is classified `Deploys as: backend process` in the Unit Table (grouped with U1–U3), but its own notes say "runs jobs through the job runner process but owns the code." ### Summary

The unit decomposition is structurally sound: the edge block is well-formed and acyclic, every ADR boundary from Domain Design is respected, entity ownership stays with one unit per entity, and story/traceability coverage is complete and arithmetically consistent. The two Major findings are real implementation risks worth the human's attention before approval — U4's split deployment story (backend process vs. job runner) is unresolved, and U7's XL size concentrates the project's own flagged highest-risk, least-familiar work into one unit that may not fit a single Bolt. Neither invalidates the topology or blocks Construction from starting, so this is READY with those two points flagged for the approval gate.
