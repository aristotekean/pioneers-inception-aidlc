### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound — symmetric dependencies, an acyclic graph, unique entity ownership, valid references, correct infra/component classification, and complete traceability with no gaps. The two hidden-coupling points the dispatch flagged (Terrain3D→Ingestion's Job, Catalog's Feature carrying Terrain3D-written `heightM`) are both disclosed with explicit ADRs and trade-off reasoning, and the row-policy visibility mechanism is properly deferred to Contract Design rather than prematurely fixed. The one substantive gap (R-01) is that the design silently resolves a real tension between FR1.2/FR4.5 (delete rights) without recording that resolution as a decision — worth the human's attention before approving, though it is a documentation/traceability gap rather than a structural flaw, and the remaining findings are minor polish items for Contract Design's benefit.
