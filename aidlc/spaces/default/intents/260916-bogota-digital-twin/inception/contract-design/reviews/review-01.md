### Summary

The contract set is internally consistent where it matters most: every OpenAPI `$ref` resolves within its own document, status/error codes are coherent with the shared conventions, and the row-policy mechanism (`SET LOCAL app.user_id` scoped to the request transaction, never on a bare pooled connection) is a sound, standard-idiom fix for the pooling risk ADR-002 flagged, and it correctly sidesteps that risk by having the backend — not an off-the-shelf tile server — serve tiles. The four spot-checked acceptance criteria (AC1.1.2, AC2.7.1, AC2.10.1, AC4.9.1) are all satisfiable by the specified endpoints and status codes. The main concern for the human at the gate is traceability drift introduced by the Q2 answer: retiring the standalone tile server invalidates U5's unit definition in `unit-of-work.md` (R-01), and C3's consumer list adds an edge (U11 → U3) the passed dependency DAG does not contain (R-02) — both are the kind of gap that should be reconciled before or at Delivery Planning rather than carried forward silently, even though neither is severe enough by itself to block this stage.
