### Summary

The wireframes and user flow are well grounded: every must-have backlog unit (PU-01 through PU-05) has a corresponding screen or flow, every confirmed Q&A answer (Q1–Q8) is reflected correctly and cited, out-of-scope items (login, datasets page, geocoding, private data) are correctly withheld, and the UI language and neutral styling match the confirmed answers. The one Major gap is that the confirmed "desktop and tablet" device commitment (Q6) is not reflected anywhere in the screens, states, or accessibility notes, which are written exclusively for keyboard/mouse desktop interaction — a real ambiguity for whoever implements the tablet form factor. The remaining findings are Minor completeness gaps (inconsistent accessibility-note landmark coverage across screens, inconsistent state coverage across screens, one unsourced UI element) that the human should weigh but do not block engineering from starting.
